The public derived database is network.json.gz. It is available at:
/assets/geography/network.json.gz

The corresponding approximate public map is basemap.geojson. The manifest.json
file records source URLs, extraction queries, snapshot dates, SHA-256 checksums,
coverage, derivation versions, assumptions and known missing connections.
The routing-only sanitized OSM source is retained under source/ for offline
rebuilding. Business names, operators, brands, contacts, address tags, free-text
notes and contributor metadata are removed. Only routing tags, topology and
route-stop names are retained; this extract is not the unmodified OSM database.

No generated person, employer, worksite, address, contact or employment-history
records are included in these public reference assets. References to real
streets and stations do not assert occupancy of any real building.
